IT ControlLens

IT ControlLens is an IT control assessment workspace designed to support Information Technology General Controls (ITGC) reviews across multiple technology environments.

Non-Intrusive Assessment: The platform helps auditors organize evidence requirements, configure testing policies, and review offline evidence. IT ControlLens is designed around offline evidence review and does not directly connect to, modify, configure, or execute commands against the systems being reviewed.
